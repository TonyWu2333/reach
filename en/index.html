AI has learned to think, but it still can't touch your life. Your schedule, your saved links and your notes sit scattered across apps that don't talk to each other. Your agent can't see them, and it can't reach them.

Reach gives your agent hands. Every Reach app is a tentacle that reaches into one corner of your life, and every tentacle connects back to the same brain: the agent you already use.

That's where the name comes from. In Chinese, Reach is 可及, from 触手可及: within reach.

We believe good tools should put what you can do at hand, not keep you stuck where you are. Between people, agents and tools, no more walls.

Tony WuCreator of Reach · November 2026

Reach List is the first hand we've reached out. Every update goes into the changelog, the whole process happens in the open, and all of the code is open source.

A lunar calendar, an Eisenhower matrix, routines and one-line quick add. A light, good-looking planner, and your agent can read and edit your tasks too.

A calendar that knows Chinese holidays

Lunar dates, solar terms and festivals right in the month view, with public holidays and make-up workdays marked.

Eisenhower matrix

Put any task in a quadrant of important × urgent. Long-press and drag to change its priority.

One-line quick add

Type “明天下午3点开会” and it's a meeting tomorrow at 15:00; “每周五晚上8点” repeats every Friday. Quick add understands Chinese for now.

Routines

Every repeating task on one page, grouped by rule, with progress rings and streaks.

Subtasks and reminders

Subtasks are full tasks. Exact local reminders, scheduled 30 days ahead.

Gestures and undo

Swipe right to complete, left to postpone, drop or delete. Every slip can be undone.

What's on my plate today?

planner today

[ ] 07:00 Morning run, 5 km  (daily)  #a1b2
[ ] 10:00 Product weekly  (urgent & important)  #c3d4
[ ] 15:30 Review designs  (important)  #e5f6
[ ] Renew domain  (overdue · yesterday)  #0a1b

Three things today, and “Renew domain” was due yesterday. Move it to today?

Move it to 4 pm today, and add badminton every Friday at 8 pm

planner edit 0a1b --date 2026-11-16 --time 16:00

planner add 每周五 Badminton --time 20:00

Done. “Renew domain” is now today at 16:00, and “Badminton” repeats every Friday at 20:00.

  1. 1

    Run the sync server

    A single-file Node server with no dependencies; your data is one JSON file. It prints a connection code when it starts.

    node planner-server.mjs
  2. 2

    Paste the code on your phone

    Open sync settings in the app, switch it on and paste the connection code.

    http://host:port#token
  3. 3

    Give your agent the skill

    Drop the skill folder into your agent's skills directory and log in once.

    ~/.claude/skills/planner
